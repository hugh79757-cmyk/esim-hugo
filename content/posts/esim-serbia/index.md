## eSIM vs Physical SIM vs Roaming

![esim-serbia](https://pub-2f5c7af1c303419a933069212bc25874.r2.dev/etap/esim-serbia/body_5.jpg)


When traveling, you typically have three options for mobile connectivity: using an eSIM, a physical SIM card, or relying on international roaming.

An eSIM offers the flexibility of switching between different carriers without needing to physically change SIM cards. This can be particularly advantageous for travelers who may visit multiple countries or require different plans for varying data needs.

Physical SIM cards, while still widely used, require you to physically replace your existing SIM, which can be inconvenient and may lead to potential loss or damage. Additionally, purchasing a local SIM card in Serbia may involve navigating language barriers and finding a provider that meets your needs.

On the other hand, international roaming with your home carrier can be expensive. Many carriers charge high fees for data usage abroad, which can quickly add up. Using an eSIM can provide a more cost-effective solution, allowing you to choose a plan that aligns with your data usage and budget.
