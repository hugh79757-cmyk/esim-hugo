Installing an eSIM is a straightforward process, but it requires a compatible device. Before you travel to Mayotte, ensure your smartphone or tablet supports eSIM functionality. Once you have purchased your eSIM plan, you will typically receive a QR code via email or through a mobile app.

To install your eSIM, follow these steps:

- Go to Settings: On your device, navigate to the settings menu.
- Select Cellular or Mobile Data: Find the option for cellular or mobile data settings.
- Add Cellular Plan: Choose the option to add a new cellular plan.
- Scan QR Code: Use your device’s camera to scan the QR code provided with your eSIM purchase.
- Follow Prompts: Complete the setup by following the on-screen instructions, which may include naming your plan and selecting it as your primary data source.

Once installed, you can activate the eSIM upon your arrival in Mayotte, ensuring you have immediate access to mobile data.

## eSIM vs Physical SIM vs Roaming

![esim-mayotte](https://pub-2f5c7af1c303419a933069212bc25874.r2.dev/etap/esim-mayotte/body_5.jpg)


When considering your options for staying connected in Mayotte, it’s important to weigh the differences between eSIMs, physical SIM cards, and roaming services.

Physical SIM cards require you to purchase a local SIM upon arrival and physically swap it with your existing SIM. This process can be cumbersome, especially if you need to keep your original number active for calls or messages. Additionally, finding a local SIM provider may take time and effort.

Roaming services, while convenient, often come with high fees. Many travelers find that international roaming charges can lead to unexpected costs, particularly if they are not aware of their data usage.

In contrast, eSIMs offer flexibility, allowing you to activate a plan without needing to change your physical SIM. This means you can easily switch between plans if your data needs change during your stay. Overall, eSIMs provide a modern and efficient solution for travelers looking to stay connected in Mayotte.
